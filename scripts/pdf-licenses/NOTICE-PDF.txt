PDF distribution notices
========================

PDF.js 6.5.438 is distributed under Apache-2.0; @napi-rs/canvas 1.1.0
is distributed under MIT. These labels describe the top-level packages,
not every font, codec, or native dependency included in their distributions.

The product preserves the shipped PDF.js LICENSE and all license files under
cmaps/, standard_fonts/, wasm/, and iccs/. The canvas wrapper LICENSE and the
target native package's sbom.cdx.json are also preserved with the binaries.

NOTICE-canvas-skia-1.1.0.txt preserves Skia and vendored dependency terms.
NOTICE-canvas-rust-1.1.0.txt conservatively includes notices for all Cargo
components listed in the native SBOM, including build dependencies. The
sources-*.json records identify the exact source archives/revisions and
checksums from which notices were collected. Identical complete license text
is printed once and referenced from subsequent component notices.

The Rust dependency set includes MPL-2.0 components (cssparser,
cssparser-macros, dtoa-short, and selectors). Their unmodified corresponding
source is available from the exact public source archive URLs in
sources-canvas-rust-1.1.0.json. Those records inform recipients where to
obtain the source; the complete MPL-2.0 terms are retained in the notices.
No source changes to these dependencies are made by XunJie.

Native npm archives are selected by the product target and verified against
the root package-lock.json SHA512 integrity before extraction. The product
records the selected native package and integrity in app/pdf-runtime.json;
all retained application/dependency/license files are covered by the product
distribution.json entryHashes. No npm executable or download is needed at
runtime. Windows x64 loading still requires validation on the target system.
